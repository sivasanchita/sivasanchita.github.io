Featured project

Youth-Friendly North Shore

A three-year, federally funded initiative led by the District of North Vancouver to build healthy, resilient communities for youth and improve the system of care. My work spanned research and community engagement.

Featured project

The ABCs of Youth Substance Use

A Ministry of Health–funded initiative promoting evidence-based substance use education in schools across Canada. I ran province-wide communities of practice that brought education and health stakeholders together, and led a knowledge translation effort to turn current evidence into practical resources for the education and health sectors.

  • The initiative The ABCs of Youth Substance Use Evidence-based substance use education for schools. Visit the website
