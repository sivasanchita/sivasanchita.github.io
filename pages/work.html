Featured project

Youth-Friendly North Shore

Youth Services Evaluation Instrument and Guide

Part of Youth-Friendly North Shore, a federally funded three-year initiative strengthening upstream systems of care for youth. A strengths-based self-evaluation tool with 40+ criteria that any organization serving youth can use, from community members and schools to municipalities and health partners. Co-developed with the community and piloted locally.

Where it started

The system from the ground up, in India.
