Hi, I'm Sanchita.

I'm a community-based researcher. I help decision-makers ensure their recommendations are evidence-backed and shaped by both research and community voices.

I believe health is built upstream, through the systems and communities we all share.

  • Vancouver, BC
  • MPH, UBC

  • English · Hindi · a little French

Values

What my work is based on

  • Community connection

    I do research with communities, not just about them. I use participatory, inclusive engagement to bring a diverse range of stakeholders together, through focus groups, surveys and sharing circles.

  • Upstream prevention

    I work to address what shapes health before people need care, because health is built through the systems and communities we share.

  • Evidence-informed decisions

    I use mixed-methods research to measure what matters, in ways that are both research-backed and community-centred.
